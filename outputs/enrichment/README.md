# Enrichment Figures — RNA-seq Analysis
Below are the enrichment-related figures generated by the pipeline. Each image has a short explanation.
---
### Heatmap NES Hallmark

![Heatmap NES Hallmark](Heatmap_NES_Hallmark.png)
Heatmap of **normalized enrichment scores (NES)** from FGSEA for Hallmark gene sets across contrasts. Red indicates positive enrichment (upregulated), blue indicates negative enrichment (downregulated). Rows are gene sets; columns are contrasts; clustering highlights groups of pathways that respond similarly.


### Violin Hallmark COAGULATION shared

![Violin Hallmark COAGULATION shared](Violin_HALLMARK_COAGULATION_shared.png)
Per-sample distribution of the **COAGULATION** Hallmark score across all groups. Wider violins indicate larger variability; median lines mark central tendency, useful to compare group-level shifts.
